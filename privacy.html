Cove Field Guide is designed to keep your field notes on your iPhone.

Effective October 6, 2026 · Version 1.0

Data stored on your device

Journal titles, notes, dates, selected photos, and any location pin you explicitly add are stored in the app's local storage. Cove does not upload this journal to a developer server. It does not require an account and contains no advertising, analytics, tracking SDK, or in-app purchase.

Photos and camera

You can choose photos with Apple's photo picker or grant camera access to take a journal photo. Cove redraws imported images before saving them as JPEG files, removing the original EXIF and GPS metadata from its saved copy. Original images in your photo library are not changed. The app does not analyze photos to identify species or assess edibility.

Optional location

Location is off by default. The app requests permission and a current position only when you choose to add a location pin. You can use the guide and journal without granting permission, remove saved pins, and revoke permission in iOS Settings. Cove does not track your route or request background location. If you choose to open a pin in an external maps app, its coordinates are passed to that app, whose privacy policy then applies.

Retention and deletion

You can edit or delete individual journal entries and remove saved photos or pins. Deleting an entry removes the app's associated local photo copies. The journal folder is excluded from device cloud backup. Removing the app or losing the device can permanently remove your records; there is no developer-held copy or recovery account.

External services

Cited source links open only when you choose them, in your browser. Those websites may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address. iOS and Apple's diagnostic services are governed by your Apple settings and Apple's policies. Cove does not run its own diagnostic collection service.

Contacting support

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send, including your email address, and use it to respond to your request. Do not include precise foraging locations or private journal records unless you want to share them. Contact jacob@hoppingdistribution.com with questions or a request to delete support correspondence.

This website

This static support site uses no analytics, forms, embedded media, or cookies set by us. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; the hosting provider may process standard request logs under GitHub's privacy statement. Website hosting is separate from the app's local journal.

Changes

If a future release changes these practices, this page will be updated with a new effective date. Material changes that need permission will be presented in the app before the relevant feature is enabled.
