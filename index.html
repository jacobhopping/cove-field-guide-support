Cove Field Guide

Notice more. Keep a record of the woods.

A regional observation guide and private field journal for iPhone, inspired by mushrooms, acorns, walnuts, and the quiet discoveries along a trail.

First release in preparation

Get help

Email jacob@hoppingdistribution.com for app help, accessibility feedback, or a correction to the guide.

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of what happened. You do not need to send your journal or location. Remove personal details from any screenshot you choose to share.

Your journal stays with you

The app stores journal entries and processed photos on your iPhone. Location is optional and requested only when you choose to add a pin. There is no app account, advertising, tracking, subscription, or in-app purchase.

Offline by design

Bundled guide content and saved journal entries work without an internet connection. Opening a cited website, sending a support email, or choosing to open a pin in another maps app requires that other service.

Keep your records safe

The journal folder is excluded from device cloud backup. Removing the app or losing your device can permanently remove your entries. Cove does not provide cloud synchronization or a recovery account.

A guide to observing

Cove does not identify a specimen from a photo and cannot determine whether it is safe to eat. Never eat a mushroom or plant based on this app. Identification features are prompts for closer observation, not a complete identification method. Consult a qualified local expert and obtain landowner permission before collecting.

If someone may have swallowed a poisonous mushroom or plant in the United States, contact Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222. Call 911 for a medical emergency.
